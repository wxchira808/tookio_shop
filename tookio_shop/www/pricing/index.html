Frequently Asked Questions

Can I upgrade my plan at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade your plan at any time. The changes will be reflected immediately, and you'll only pay the prorated difference for the current billing period.
What happens if I exceed my plan limits?
If you approach your plan limits, we'll notify you and suggest upgrading to the next tier. You won't be charged extra, but you may need to upgrade to continue adding items or invoices.
Is there a free trial available?
Yes, we offer a 14-day free trial for all paid plans. You can start with any plan and explore all features before committing.
How does the Enterprise plan work?
Our Enterprise plan is fully customized based on your specific needs. Contact our sales team to discuss your requirements and get a custom quote.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime?
Yes, you can cancel your subscription at any time. Your account will remain active until the end of your current billing period.
