Privacy Policy

Effective date: January 6, 2026

Tookio Shop helps sellers manage inventory, sales, invoices, purchases and account activity. This policy explains what we collect and how we protect it.

Information we collect

  • Account details such as name, email address and phone number.
  • Business records you create in Tookio Shop, including products, shops, purchases and sales invoices.
  • Usage and diagnostic information used to keep the service reliable.

How we use information

We use your information to operate Tookio Shop, support your account, improve the product, secure the service and communicate important service updates.

Data sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We only share information when needed to operate the service, comply with law or protect Tookio Shop and its users.

Security

We use access controls, authentication and structured permissions to reduce unauthorized access to your business data.

Your rights

You may request account updates or deletion. Account deletion disables access and removes your ability to use Tookio Shop services.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact info@tookio.co.ke.
