Built for social sellers in Kenya and beyond.

Tookio Shop started from a simple observation: selling online through Instagram, WhatsApp, and TikTok is a great way to build a business, but keeping track of everything gets messy very fast.

Between unread DMs, misplaced receipts, lost stock counts, and end-of-month calculations, sellers spend too much time managing logistics instead of serving customers and growing their reach.

We built Tookio Shop to be the simple, reliable backbone for your online store. It is not a complex enterprise ERP, and it is not a bloated e-commerce site builder. It is exactly what you need to track what came in, what went out, and what money you made today.
